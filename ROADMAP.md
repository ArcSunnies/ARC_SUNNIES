# ArcSunnies ($ASN) — Community Roadmap

> Roadmap milestones are goals, not guarantees. Timelines may change based on community feedback, technical readiness, and available resources.

## Phase 01 — Brand & Foundations (Now)
- Publish the ArcSunnies identity and landing page.
- Add official social/community channels once confirmed.
- Share clear project information and regular updates.
- Keep all contract and launch details transparent.

## Phase 02 — Meme Community
- Run meme contests, fan-art prompts, and creative campaigns.
- Feature community-created content with permission.
- Build community guidelines and anti-scam reminders.
- Encourage participation without promising financial rewards.

## Phase 03 — Token Launch & Listing Preparation (Planned)
- Confirm blockchain/network, token contract, supply, and token mechanics.
- Publish the contract address only after deployment and independent verification.
- Publish token distribution and risk information.
- Explore listing options after launch readiness is confirmed.

## Phase 04 — Sunnies NFTs (Coming Soon)
- Explore collection concepts and invite community feedback.
- Finalize art, supply, pricing, mint process, and any intended utility.
- Publish contract and security details before a mint.
- Launch only when the collection is ready; no NFT availability is implied today.

## Phase 05 — Staking & Rewards (Coming Soon)
- Research whether staking is feasible and appropriate.
- Define eligibility, lockups, reward source, risks, and mechanics.
- Obtain appropriate technical/security review before launch.
- Publish clear documentation before users can participate.
- **No staking or rewards are live, and future rewards are not guaranteed.**

## Phase 06 — Long-term Community Ecosystem
- Explore collaborations, creator events, community tools, and other digital experiences.
- Prioritize ideas based on sustained community interest and practical feasibility.
- Publish progress and changes openly.
